Mandelbrot explorer

An interactive explorer for the Mandelbrot set. It renders in your browser on your graphics card with WebGL 2, and uses perturbation theory with arbitrary-precision arithmetic to zoom far beyond the limits of 64-bit floating point. Any view can be downloaded as a high-resolution PNG.
